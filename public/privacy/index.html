Privacy Policy

Owner and contact

Roamark is owned by Naman Sinha. Contact naman@roamark.in for privacy questions, corrections, deletion requests or grievances. Effective 11 October 2026.

Information we collect

When accounts are configured, we collect your email address, display name, Google profile image when available, saved-place identifiers and reviews. Reviews include visit month, travel context, ratings, verdict and text. Authentication uses Google OAuth and hashed session tokens. Google supplies your basic profile, account identifier, profile image and verified email; we do not receive your Google password. Your date of birth is submitted to check age before Google registration. It is used for that eligibility check and is not stored in your account or published. The current service accepts adult account registration only.

Purposes

We process information to operate accounts, verify Google account identity, save places, publish moderated opinions, prevent abuse and handle requests. With your permission, Google Analytics 4 measures page visits, device/browser information and approximate geography to improve the service. Analytics is not loaded until you accept. We do not send promotional emails.

Public and private data

Published reviews expose display name and travel context, not email addresses. Pending reviews appear in your own profile. Email verification does not establish a visit. Optional review photos and videos are collected when you upload them. GPS access and independent visit evidence are not currently collected.

Providers and transfers

MongoDB Atlas stores account data when configured. Your deployment host processes service requests, and Google handles account authentication under its own privacy policy. Google Analytics receives usage data only after acceptance; Google may process it outside India under its own privacy terms. We do not intentionally send review text, email addresses, account IDs, URL query strings or fragments to Analytics. Analytics is excluded from account, admin and review-form pages. Google Fonts and Unsplash receive requests for fonts and imagery; linked map and tourism sites follow their own policies. Providers may process data outside India. Hosting region depends on the operator configuration.

Cookies and storage

A secure, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookie supports authentication in production. Sessions expire after seven days. The older browser shortlist may remain on a device but is not a verified account record. Clear browser site data to remove legacy local records. Optional Google Analytics cookies (including _ga) are used only after acceptance. Your choice is saved in local storage. Use Cookie settings to accept or withdraw consent; withdrawal stops future measurement and clears accessible Analytics cookies. Essential authentication remains available if you reject analytics.

Retention

OTP challenges expire after ten minutes and verification is attempt-limited; MongoDB expiry cleanup can occur after logical expiration. Sessions expire after seven days. Rate-limit records expire after their enforcement period. Account details, saved places and reviews remain until deleted or needed for service operation. Provider backups and independently retained host logs follow provider arrangements; a finalized backup retention schedule remains to be established before broad public launch.

Your control

Delete your account and associated reviews from your profile, or contact naman@roamark.in. Deletion removes active account records, sessions, saved places, reviews and outstanding email challenges. Requests for access, correction, consent withdrawal or grievances may require proportionate identity checks. Deletion does not promise immediate erasure from provider backups or legally required records.

Teenagers and children

The intended community starts at age 14. Under-14 accounts are unavailable. Ages 14–17 cannot register until an appropriate guardian-consent process is supported. No targeted advertising or behavioral analytics for children is enabled. Report suspected inappropriate child-data collection to the contact address.

Advertising and affiliates

No ad network or affiliate program is integrated. Advertising may be introduced only with updated disclosures and appropriate controls. No promotional email program is active. Sponsorship must be disclosed and must not silently alter ratings.

Security

Google authorization uses short-lived state and PKCE; Google passwords and long-lived Google tokens are not stored. Sessions use random tokens stored as hashes, and production cookies are Secure and HttpOnly. Database access and production secrets must be configured securely by the host operator. No absolute security guarantee is made. Applicable incident-notification obligations will be assessed if an incident occurs.

Rights, complaints and policy changes

Contact Naman Sinha at naman@roamark.in. Statutory privacy and complaint rights remain available where applicable. Material changes will be dated and notified where required. Features mentioned as future possibilities do not authorize new data collection automatically. This policy requires review against the actual deployment, provider arrangements and applicable law before public launch.

Review photos

Optional photos are stored with your review in MongoDB. Published review photos are public; pending photos are visible through your account. Upload only images you have permission to share and avoid private details or identifiable people without consent. Account deletion removes your reviews and photos from the active database. Copies may remain in backups until their retention period ends.

Itinerary drafts and embedded videos

The itinerary builder stores your plan in local browser storage on your current device. It is not synced to your account or sent to Roamark. Anyone using that browser profile may be able to access it. Clearing browser storage removes the draft. Videos uploaded by administrators or reviewers are stored and optimized by Cloudinary. Visitors who play those videos contact Cloudinary. The original is retained separately. Unsubmitted or pending review videos do not appear in the public review feed. Removing a video from a draft, archiving a contribution or deleting an account removes its display or database references; cloud originals are not automatically deleted and require administrator cleanup. YouTube videos are loaded only when you choose to play them, at which point your browser contacts YouTube and its privacy policies apply.

Contribution votes

We store your account ID with votes on reviews and photos to prevent duplicate votes. Public pages show totals, not voter identities. Votes help order contributions and are separate from destination ratings. You may remove a vote. Account deletion removes your votes and votes attached to your deleted reviews.
