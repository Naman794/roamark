Travel has a feeling. Find yours.

Roamark began with a question that travel photos do not always answer: does this place have the vibe I am looking for?

A traveler’s idea

I’m Naman Sinha, the owner of Roamark. I love traveling and exploring new places. But sometimes it is difficult to understand what a place will actually feel like before you arrive. That uncertainty is what brought Roamark into existence.

The idea is simple: a place where travelers share their own points of view and thoughts, helping others identify the right places to visit.

More than a beautiful photograph

A destination can feel peaceful to one person and crowded to another. The season, company, budget and expectations all matter. We want to make that context visible, alongside what people enjoyed and what disappointed them.

How we see a place

Wonder, Breathing Room, Local Soul and Worth the Effort form our feeling profile. A return verdict and a traveler’s own words add the context a single score cannot provide.

A community built on honest perspectives

We welcome respectful disagreement. We want specific personal experiences, clear commercial disclosures and useful corrections. Verified email and evidence of a visit will be separate signals, not promises that every opinion is true.

Where we are today

Roamark is growing its India directory. Place records awaiting verification are labeled. Google login verifies account access, not a visit. Reviews are stored in MongoDB and await moderation before publication. Some destination guides contain editorial suggestions rather than live operational information.

Who it is for

Our intended community starts at age 14, with no upper age limit. Under-18 account participation will require an appropriate guardian-consent process before live accounts are enabled.
