Kerala

KNOW THE PLACE BEFORE THE JOURNEY

Destination overview

Alleppey, officially Alappuzha, is one of Kerala’s best-known backwater destinations. Its waterways are part of a wider coastal district with community life, religious centers, food traditions and cultural events. Kerala Tourism places particular emphasis on the district’s water-based heritage.

Boat journeys and waterside stays are only one way to experience the area. A visit can also include the coast and local cultural places, depending on interests and available time. Compare the route and facilities of a boat booking instead of treating every backwater trip as the same product.

Food & the local table

Kerala meals, rice-based dishes and local cooking offer plenty to explore. Confirm dietary preferences and exactly which meals your boat booking includes.

A flexible starter itinerary
  1. Arrive, check your embarkation arrangements and enjoy a relaxed local meal.
  2. Take your chosen boat experience, keeping the evening unhurried.
  3. Add a local or coastal stop if conditions allow, then depart.
Access, etiquette & packing

Access: Boat boarding can involve steps and narrow walkways. Confirm boarding assistance, onboard toilets and accessible alternatives before booking.

Local etiquette: Waterways are working and residential spaces. Keep waste off the water and ask before photographing residents.

Bring along: Light clothing, rain protection, sun protection, a dry bag and suitable footwear for boarding.
