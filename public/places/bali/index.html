Destination overview

Bali combines beaches, inland landscapes, temples and cultural traditions within one Indonesian island destination. Indonesia’s official tourism guide highlights experiences that range from the coast to ceremonial and religious sites, including the sea temple at Tanah Lot.

The island should not be treated as one compact neighborhood. A coast-focused holiday and a cultural or inland stay can involve different bases and transfers. Choose experiences around your interests and respect the rules of active religious places rather than planning only around photo opportunities.

Explore the details

Editorial suggestions. Check operating hours, prices and access directly before booking.

Experiences to explore
  • Ubud arts and cultural stops — Allow time for local performances and workshops rather than only a photo stop.
  • Rice-field landscapes — Use permitted paths and respect working land.
  • Temple visits — Confirm access and dress rules for each site.
  • Coastal day — Select a beach for conditions and facilities, not only appearance.
Choose your base
  • Ubud — an inland base for arts and cultural exploration.
  • Sanur — a coastal option to compare for a more relaxed stay.
  • Southern coastal areas — choose by beach access, traffic and the atmosphere you want.
Food & the local table

Explore Indonesian and Balinese dishes, asking about ingredients, spice and dietary needs. Check food handling and drinking-water arrangements.

A flexible starter itinerary
  1. Arrive and settle in one base rather than immediately crossing the island.
  2. Give one day to local culture and nearby landscapes.
  3. Choose a coast day or a workshop, leaving a buffer for transfers.
Access, etiquette & packing

Access: Footpaths, temple steps and rural paths differ considerably. Request specific accessibility information from stays and tour operators.

Local etiquette: Respect offerings, temple rules and ceremonies. Do not enter restricted sacred spaces for photographs.

Bring along: Light clothing, rain protection, sun protection and suitable clothing for cultural visits.
